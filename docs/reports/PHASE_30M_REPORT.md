## Pause Diagnosis

The unchanged Test 1 text enters the existing production path as two outer
chunks, of 154 and 183 characters, ending at sentence periods. The production
joiner fills only a measured deficit and targets at most 130 ms at a period
boundary. Therefore it cannot by itself create the observed 330–418 ms silent
runs in M-A. The audio evidence is `MIXED`: longer-than-250 ms runs occur in all
identities, including K-A and M-C, so the current WAVs do not establish an
M-A-only identity defect; generated within-chunk silence/cadence is a plausible
contributor. Exact word-to-silence alignment is unavailable without adding a
transcription dependency, which this phase does not do.

## Pause Measurements

12.705 s, 256 ms at 13.181 s, and 330 ms at 16.284 s. Its two 330 ms-or-longer
