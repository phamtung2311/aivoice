## Review protocol used

Do not open `blind_mapping.json` before finishing the review. Listen to `blind/01.wav` through `blind/04.wav` without trying to infer which pause policy each file uses.

1. Which version finally stops feeling rushed?
2. Which gives enough breathing space between ideas?
3. Which preserves the good semantic emphasis?
4. Which feels most like a real podcast narrator?
5. Which is easiest to listen to for 20–30 minutes?
6. Which starts to feel too slow or over-paused?
7. Most importantly: does the listener now have enough time to absorb an emphasized idea before the next one begins?

Human listening was authoritative; the winner was not selected from duration or signal metrics alone.
