# Reference contract — Meme / Giọng Gió

Place an intentionally supplied, permissioned WAV at:

`experiments/special_voice_meme/reference_audio/meme_breath.wav`

Use approximately 6–8 seconds of one Vietnamese speaker. The recording should
be mono-compatible, articulate and naturally airy/breathy or distinctive in its
vocal delivery. It must not rely on music, effects, echo, clipping, heavy room
reverb, microphone noise, or extreme whispering that loses consonants.

This experiment tests reference-conditioned voice character only. It will not
add pitch shifting, speed tricks, EQ, synthetic breaths, or post-processing.
