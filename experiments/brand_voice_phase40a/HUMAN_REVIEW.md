Listen directly in Vietnamese to exactly the four files in `audio/`.

Classify each candidate as `NO`, `INTERESTING`, or `YES` based on:

1. distinctive person versus generic TTS preset;
2. medium-low depth and natural vocal body;
3. force and semantic focus;
4. philosophical/reflective podcast fit;
5. naturalness without morph, drift, metallic texture, doubled voice, or slurring;
6. comfort for 20–30 minutes;
7. whether it feels more like this channel's potential voice than an industrial preset.

`INTERESTING` requires a clearly identifiable useful acoustic fingerprint, not
merely acceptable quality. Only a clear `YES`, or a genuinely distinctive
`INTERESTING`, can justify a separately approved next phase.
