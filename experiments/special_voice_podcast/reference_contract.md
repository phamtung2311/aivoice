# Podcast reference contract

Use only an audio reference supplied or explicitly approved by the user. Do not
download, scrape, or clone a creator, public figure, podcast, YouTube, or TikTok
voice without that permission.

Place the primary WAV at:

`experiments/special_voice_podcast/reference_audio/podcast_reference.wav`

Additional independently permissioned candidates may be named
`candidate_01.wav`, `candidate_02.wav`, and so on. One file represents one
possible brand voice. Never merge, mix, or average different speakers.

## Ideal initial clip

- 6–8 seconds of usable Vietnamese speech from one speaker
- A clean, mono-compatible recording without music, effects, clipping, or
  significant room reverb/noise
- Natural speaking delivery, clear consonants, a warm/intimate quality, natural
  emphasis, phrase-level rhythm, at least one useful pause, and a natural ending
- Approximately one or two complete sentences with reflective, storytelling,
  thoughtful, or conversational semantic style

Choose delivery rather than merely low fundamental pitch. Avoid clips dominated
by shouting, whispering, laughing, crying, singing, advertising, newsreader
delivery, or movie-trailer exaggeration.

The local validator records duration, sample rate, channels, peak, RMS,
clipping, leading/trailing silence, and its lightweight noise warning. It reports
clearly unsuitable material instead of silently repairing severe defects.
