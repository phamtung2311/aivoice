# Podcast Brand Voice specification

This specification defines the target independently of any individual recording.
It is not a request to make a generic narrator or simply the deepest possible
male TTS voice.

## Identity

The intended Vietnamese voice is deep without sounding artificially low, warm,
full-bodied, slightly intimate, clear and recognizably its own. It must not be
thin, harsh, muddy, excessively breathy, or dependent on audio effects for its
character.

## Delivery and rhythm

It speaks with calm confidence and conversational narration, using controlled
expressiveness, clear phrase grouping, natural emphasis, sentence endings, and
micro-pauses. Its pace belongs between medium-slow and medium: never sluggish
or rushed. Important phrases should receive more weight than supporting ones.

## Emotional range

The reference-conditioned delivery should naturally support reflective, curious,
serious, gentle, and slightly dramatic moments without overacting. This project
does not claim explicit, independent emotion controls.

## Long-form standard

For a 10–30 minute episode, the voice must remain intelligible, varied, and
comfortable. Excess bass, theatrical emphasis, repetitive cadence, robotic
rhythm, and sustained vocal fatigue are failures.

## Brand distinctiveness

The goal is a recognizable channel identity, not merely “a good male TTS
voice.” Listening evaluation therefore includes distinctiveness, memorability,
podcast suitability, and brand-voice potential. These are human listening
criteria, not mathematical claims of recognition.

## Non-goals for this phase

Do not fake depth with pitch/formant shifting, bass boost, EQ, speed reduction,
reverb, compression, or any mastering trick. Do not tune sampling or run a
parameter search. Start with native VieNeu production defaults and speed 1.0.

Future production may optionally use loudness normalization, gentle compression,
noise-floor management, and limiting; mastering is separate from voice identity
and must never hide poor synthesis.
