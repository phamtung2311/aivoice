# Phase 30E decision

**Status: `HUMAN_REFERENCE_REQUIRED`**

The installed VieNeu system has no legitimate new-speaker mechanism without a
reference. The reviewed local voice-design alternatives lack verified Vietnamese
support or have licensing/hardware barriers. Phase 30F should acquire one
purpose-recorded, permissioned 6–8 second Vietnamese reference with the desired
warm, distinctive, broadly understandable podcast delivery, then validate it
through the existing Phase 30B scaffold.
