# Casting-source comparison

## Primary source: Mozilla Common Voice Vietnamese (conditional)

This is the best **technical** free pool because it combines many speakers, anonymous source IDs, optional broad metadata, a permissive listed dataset license, and a modest footprint. It is not production-approved: short crowd-recorded read prompts vary in microphone/room quality, and the privacy condition makes branded, speaker-specific synthesis an unresolved use.

Observed v23 estimate: 413.72 MB compressed, 18,777 clips, 22 total hours / 6.3 validated, 361 speakers. No download occurred. After approval, expect roughly 0.4–0.5 GB archive and 1–2 GB working space for extraction, manifests, and audition derivatives; confirm on release day.

## Secondary source: NONE

VIVOS has conflicting license claims; ViSpeech has no clear audio license evidence; VieNeu and Dolly are non-commercial. Web/social/podcast content is not a fallback.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Speaker diversity | Strong technical pool; start with 20–50 anonymous IDs, then narrow to ~10 |
| Audio quality | Mixed; use strict technical filtering and listening |
| Natural podcast delivery | Weak-to-moderate; short scripted prompts cannot demonstrate long-form hosting |
| Dialect/region metadata | Optional broad filter only; never exclude Bắc/Nam/Trung labels automatically |
| Local processing | CPU-feasible, sub-GB source |
| Brand risk | High until a source-specific written review closes |
