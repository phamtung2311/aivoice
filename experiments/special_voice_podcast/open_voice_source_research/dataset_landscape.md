# Vietnamese speech-source landscape

Research date: 2026-08-31. Counts are observed release/card metadata and must be rechecked before use.

| Source | Speaker / audio signal | Rights signal | Brand-casting result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mozilla Common Voice Vietnamese v23 | 361 speakers; 18,777 MP3 clips; 22 h total / 6.3 h validated; optional age/gender/accent and anonymous client IDs | Dataset card lists CC0-1.0; download terms prohibit attempting speaker identity | Best technical pool; **UNCLEAR** for persistent branded synthetic voice |
| ViSpeech | 449 speakers; 10,686 unscripted MP3; >14 h; gender and 3 dialect labels | Repository describes corpus but no clear audio-specific license evidenced | **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE** |
| VIVOS | ~65 volunteers; 15 h; read speech / quiet-room reports | Conflicting CC-BY-SA vs CC-BY-NC-SA claims in non-authoritative listings | **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE**; likely commercial blocker |
| VieNeu TTS 500h | 193 public speakers / 140.7 public h cited on card | CC-BY-NC-4.0 | **RESTRICTED** |
| Dolly Audio | 152 speakers / ~1,000 h | CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 | **RESTRICTED** |
| viVoice, internet-derived VietTTS, web sources | Vietnamese material exists | Non-commercial or unclear web provenance | **NOT_ALLOWED** |

## Sources and exclusions

- [Mozilla Common Voice catalogue](https://commonvoice.mozilla.org/data) and its Vietnamese v23 card: counts, format, metadata, CC0 listing.
- [Common Voice download terms](https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmfzu8u8wa555eq8onrk334h4) and [Mozilla staff privacy discussion](https://discourse.mozilla.org/t/speaker-ids-for-speaker-identification-model/114358).
- [ViSpeech repository](https://github.com/TranNguyenNB/ViSpeech) for its stated 449-speaker corpus.
- [Common Voice corpus paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.520.pdf) for anonymous client-ID structure.

YouTube/TikTok/podcast scraping, celebrity speech, and “free to listen” content are excluded: public availability is not consent or a cloning grant.
