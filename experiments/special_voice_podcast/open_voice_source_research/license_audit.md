## Common Voice nuance

CC0 answers a copyright-license question. It does not cancel click-through conditions, privacy, personality/publicity rights, or prove consent to make one anonymous volunteer into a durable commercial identity. Mozilla’s published staff response specifically cautions that identification through matching/grouping contributor clips is inconsistent with robust privacy practice. Therefore this audit does not call speaker-level commercial cloning “clearly allowed.”

Before any use, archive the exact release card and terms; obtain written confirmation covering anonymous source-ID grouping, synthesis, public output, commercial use, retention/deletion, and withdrawal handling. Never seek a contributor’s real-world identity. Do not publish a recognizably derived voice without direct, specific consent and a signed talent agreement.
